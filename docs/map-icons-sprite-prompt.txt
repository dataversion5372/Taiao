MAP ICON SPRITESHEET — image generation prompt
=================================================
Purpose: replacement art for the Isle of Emberfall world-map marker icons
(currently hand-drawn Canvas2D vector glyphs, rendered at 18x18px on the map
and 10x10px on the minimap). One combined spritesheet, extracted by fixed
grid position afterward — do not rely on labels for identification.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT (send as one single image-generation request)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Create ONE flat 2D game-icon spritesheet: a precise 6-column by 5-row grid
of 30 equal square cells, generous even padding between cells so nothing
touches or overlaps its neighbors. Every cell has the exact same flat,
solid, uniform magenta background color (#FF00FF) with zero gradient,
texture, or shadow in the background — this magenta is a chroma-key color
to be removed later, so it must not appear anywhere in the icon artwork
itself. Do not draw grid lines, cell borders, labels, numbers, or captions
anywhere in the image.

Art style for every icon: simple, bold, flat-shaded fantasy RPG minimap/
world-map marker icon, similar to OSRS-style map icons — chunky readable
silhouette, thick clean black outline (this outline is important, keep it
consistent on every icon), 3-6 flat solid colors per icon (no gradients,
no soft shading, no photorealism), designed to stay legible when scaled
down to 18x18 pixels. Every icon should be centered in its cell and sized
consistently relative to the others (similar visual weight/scale, none
tiny and none overflowing).

Fill the 30 cells, reading left-to-right then top-to-bottom, with exactly
these icons in exactly this order:

Row 1: (1) a gold dollar-sign coin/bank icon, (2) a purple drawstring
merchant pouch with a yellow band, (3) a grey blacksmith's anvil, (4) a
grey stone furnace with an orange flame glowing in its mouth, (5) a small
blue fish with a black eye, (6) a pickaxe swinging with a grey arc motion
trail.

Row 2: (7) a tree with a brown trunk and a round green leafy canopy, (8)
a gold four-pointed sparkle/star with a white center dot (altar/shrine
icon), (9) a cyan four-pointed sparkle/star (quest marker, same shape as
the altar star but colored cyan-blue instead of gold), (10) a tan windmill
tower with a black-and-cream X-shaped sail blade, (11) a blue water
droplet/teardrop with a small white highlight, (12) a wooden carpenter's
workbench with a grey vice clamp on top.

Row 3: (13) a brown wooden weaver's loom frame with cream vertical threads
and a red horizontal weave line, (14) a garden flower with a green stem
and five pink petals around a yellow center, (15) a light-blue alchemist's
flask/beaker shape with green bubbling liquid inside, (16) a brown wooden
tanning rack with a tan animal hide stretched on it, (17) a dark cauldron
pot with green bubbling potion on top and a horizontal rim bar, (18) a
butcher's wooden bench with a red cut of meat and a grey cleaver above it.

Row 4: (19) a grey cooking range/stove with a black pot opening and an
orange-and-yellow flame inside, (20) a crossed black-and-silver sword with
a gold hilt, (21) a wooden bow strung vertically with a silver arrow
nocked and pointing right, (22) a brown wand with a purple four-pointed
sparkle/star at its tip, (23) a faceted red gem/diamond shape with white
highlight facet lines, (24) a green leaf-shaped herbalist pouch/sachet
with a visible leaf vein pattern.

Row 5: (25) a blue folded tunic/shirt clothing icon with short sleeves,
(26) a red round loaf of bread with a small green sprig on top, (27) a
small orange campfire with red-orange flames over crossed brown logs, (28)
a fruit tree with a brown trunk, a round green canopy, and several small
red apple-like fruit dots scattered across the canopy (distinct from icon
#7's plain, fruitless tree), (29) a wooden drying/smoking rack with two
brown strips of cured meat or fish hanging from a crossbar and a couple of
small pale grey smoke wisps rising above them, (30) leave completely empty
(nothing but the plain magenta background).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Notes for whoever runs this (not part of the prompt itself)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Icons #1-26 are 1:1 replacements for the 26 hand-drawn Canvas2D icons
  that exist today (bank, store, anvil, furnace, fish, mine, tree, altar,
  quest, windmill, water, workbench, loom, garden, alchemy, tanning,
  cauldron, butcher, range, swordshop, bowshop, magicshop, gemshop,
  herbshop, clothesshop, foodshop) — including 8 (swordshop through
  foodshop) that no current code path actually displays; kept per request
  in case named shops get wired up later.
- Icon #27 (campfire) fills a real gap: wildIcon() can already emit a
  "camp" marker type with no matching art, so it's silently invisible on
  the map today.
- Icon #28 (fruit tree) fills a real gap: "orchard" is a genuine POI type
  (grassland biomes, gets a real generated name like "X Orchard") but has
  no entry in the POI-type -> icon lookup table, so it renders with no
  icon today.
- Icon #29 (drying rack) fills a real gap: "seasoning_yard" is a real
  crafting station used by actual recipes and spawns in cities, but has
  no entry in the station -> icon lookup table, so it renders with no
  icon today.
- Cell 30 is spare/blank on purpose (30 = 6x5 grid, 29 icons used).
- Extraction will use the same border-flood-fill alpha technique used for
  prior reference sheets in this project (crop non-magenta content per
  cell, flood-fill the magenta background to transparent) — magenta was
  picked because it doesn't occur naturally in any of the 29 icon
  descriptions above.
- Once the generated sheet comes back, the plan is to: extract all 29
  icons by grid position, replace the current Canvas2D-vector iconImgs
  system with real sprite-atlas lookups, and fix the two lookup-table
  gaps (STATION_ICON.seasoning_yard, and IT.orchard in
  iconsNearForMap()) so those two icons actually start appearing.

GLOBAL STYLE (both sheets)
==============================================================================
16-bit / 'HD-2D' pixel-art inventory icons in the look of the roguelike NES RPGs
and classic RuneScape fish sprites. One item per cell, centred and filling most
of the cell, shown SIDE-ON (facing left), bold readable silhouette, thin dark
outline, a limited flat palette with light dithered shading, soft light from the
upper-left. Fully TRANSPARENT background (alpha), no drop shadows, text, numbers,

32x32 from its shape and colour. Output: one PNG, a uniform grid of 8 columns x
3 rows, 64x64 px per cell (drawn at 64x64, downscaled to 32x32 in game), one icon
per cell, even margins, no gridlines, in the EXACT order below.

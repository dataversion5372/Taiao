ISLE OF EMBERFALL — FISHER'S SOAP ICON PROMPT
=============================================
CONTEXT: Fisher's soap (gameplay/stink.js, item id "fishers_soap") is a cheap
basic soap bar sold by the fishmonger — the fisherfolk's bar for scrubbing the
reek of the catch off their hands. It currently uses a tinted i_cloth placeholder
and needs its own icon, in the same style as the game's other inventory items.

==============================================================================
STYLE
==============================================================================
A single 16-bit / 'HD-2D' pixel-art inventory icon in the look of the roguelike
NES RPGs and classic RuneScape item sprites — matching the game's other item
icons. One object, centred and filling most of the cell, drawn 3/4 or flat
side-on, bold readable silhouette, thin dark outline, a limited flat palette with
a little dithered shading, soft light from the upper-left. Fully TRANSPARENT
background (alpha), no drop shadow, text, labels, border or frame. Drawn at
64x64 px (downscaled to 32x32 in game) — must read clearly at 32x32.

==============================================================================
THE OBJECT — FISHER'S SOAP
==============================================================================
A single hand-sized BAR OF SOAP, a rounded-rectangle block with softly bevelled
edges and a slightly worn, used top. Coarse, honest, home-boiled soap — not a
fancy perfumed cake. Colour it a pale sea-foam blue-green / grey-green with a
faint cool sheen, so it reads as the "fish / sea" soap of the set. A small motif
is pressed into the top face to say what it's for: a simple stamped FISH
silhouette (or a couple of wavy sea lines) embossed into the bar. Optional tiny
details: a few coarse salt-crystal flecks catching the light, a whisper of
lather/soap-suds at one corner, a slightly softened melted edge. Keep it a plain,
sturdy fisherman's bar — cheap and practical.

INTEGRATION (after the PNG is generated):
  - Slice/pad to a 64px cell (transparent bg) and self-register in a small
    js/sprites file with a FREE 2-letter key, SHEET_TILE=64:
    SPR["i_fishers_soap"] = [key,0,0,{sx:0,sy:0,sw:64,sh:64}].
  - It already repoints via ITEMS.fishers_soap.icon = "i_fishers_soap" — just
    swap that SPR entry from the tinted-cloth placeholder to the new sheet cell
    (load the file AFTER gameplay/stink.js so the item exists).
  - Could also serve as a base for the other soaps if a full soap sheet is made
    later (the Chandlery soapmaking ladder, chandlery.js).

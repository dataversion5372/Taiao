TAIAO — MALTING ICON PROMPT
=======================================
AUDIT: the Malting skill (js/skills/fillout.js) has 32 recipes — one per grain,
"Malt <grain>" — but they all funnel into just ~6 shared malt items (malt,
dark_malt, crystal_malt, wheat_malt, rye_malt, smoked_malt), every one a
hue-tinted recolour of the base i_wheat sprite. So all 32 malting products look
alike in the pack, malthouse and skill guide. This sheet gives Malting a full
per-grain malt line: 32 distinct malts, one for each of the 32 Cerealiculture
grains (content.js GRAIN_NAMES, Wheat -> Celestial quinoa), each the KILNED
(malted) form of that grain — toasted whole kernels, glossy, ready for the mash.

==============================================================================
GLOBAL STYLE
==============================================================================
Art style: 16-bit / 'HD-2D' pixel-art inventory icons in the look of the
roguelike NES RPGs and classic RuneScape item sprites. One malt per cell,
centred and filling most of the cell: a small rounded HEAP of malted grain with
a few loose kernels scattered at the front — clearly KILNED/ROASTED whole grain
(toasted, glossy, with a little dithered sheen), NOT flour/powder, NOT a sack,
NOT a growing plant. Bold readable silhouette, thin dark outline, limited flat
palette, soft light from the upper-left. Fully TRANSPARENT background (alpha),
no drop shadows, no text, numbers, labels, containers, borders or frames.
Consistent heap scale, framing and lighting across the whole sheet so the 32
icons read as one matched malt set. Every cell is a DIFFERENT malt — tell them
apart by kernel shape/size, roast colour and any signature glow.

TWO signals distinguish the cells:
  · KERNEL CHARACTER — each grain's kernels keep their own form (barley's
    creased husked kernel, corn's big flat maize kernel, rice's polished grain,
    millet's tiny beads, quinoa's ringed disc, etc.).
  · ROAST / TIER GRADIENT — reading left-to-right, top-to-bottom the kiln
    deepens: pale straw-gold at the start, through amber and caramel, into
    brown and dark-roast; the final magical grains glow rather than merely
    darken. Keep glow subtle so the heap of kernels still carries the shape.

Output: one PNG sprite sheet, a uniform grid of 8 columns x 4 rows, 64x64 px
per cell (draw at 64x64; the game downscales to 32x32), one icon per cell, even
margins, no gridlines drawn, in the EXACT left-to-right / top-to-bottom order
below.

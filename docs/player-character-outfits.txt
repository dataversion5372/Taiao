TAIAO — PLAYER CHARACTER OUTFITS
============================================
Every selectable outfit for each of the 223 player characters (character-select
roster, js/sprites/characters-data.js CHAR_LIST, in order). Each outfit STATE a
character can wear on the C-menu (from OUTFIT_STATES / assets/families_source) is
described in enough detail to recreate it: garments, layers, colours, trim,
accessories, weapons and hair. "New Outfit" is the AI-generated alternate; the
others are the default look edited (new hair, armed, undressed, gender/feature
variants, Māori flax cloaks, etc.).

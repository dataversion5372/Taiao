TAIAO — FULLING ICON PROMPT
=======================================
AUDIT: the Fulling skill (js/skills/textiles.js) has one "Full <cloth>" recipe
for every one of the 32 woven cloths (content.js TEXTILE_NAMES) — so its
progression guide is 32+ rungs — but they all finish into just 8 shared
"finished cloth" items grouped by material class, every one sharing a single
tinted i_cloth placeholder. So most guide rows show the same handful of icons.
This sheet gives Fulling a full per-cloth line: 32 distinct FULLED cloths, one
for each woven cloth, each a bolt of that fabric washed and fulled dense,
napped and weatherproof — so every guide entry has its own icon.

==============================================================================
GLOBAL STYLE
==============================================================================
Art style: 16-bit / 'HD-2D' pixel-art inventory icons in the look of the
roguelike NES RPGs and classic RuneScape item sprites. One cloth per cell,
centred and filling most of the cell: a short BOLT of finished cloth — a neatly
folded/rolled stack of fabric shown 3/4, a couple of layers thick with a soft
fold or rolled end so it reads as finished yardage, NOT a garment, NOT loose
yarn, NOT a flat swatch. Each is washed and fulled — dense, slightly napped,
weatherproof. Bold readable silhouette, thin dark outline, limited flat palette
with a little dithered shading, soft light from the upper-left. Fully
TRANSPARENT background (alpha), no drop shadows, no text, numbers, labels,
borders or frames. Consistent bolt scale, framing and lighting across the whole
sheet so the 32 icons read as one matched set of finished cloth — every cell a
DIFFERENT fabric, tellable at a glance by colour, weave and surface finish.

TIER GRADIENT (top-left plain -> bottom-right opulent/magical): the early bolts
are humble utility cloth in naturals and earth tones; the middle bolts are fine
patterned and lustrous fabrics; the last bolts are metallic, jewelled and
enchanted — add sheen, shimmer or a faint glow to the high-tier ones so a glance
down the sheet reads as a rising value curve. Keep any glow subtle so the folded
bolt still carries the silhouette.

Output: one PNG sprite sheet, a uniform grid of 8 columns x 4 rows, 64x64 px per
cell (draw at 64x64; the game downscales to 32x32), one icon per cell, even
margins, no gridlines drawn, in the EXACT left-to-right / top-to-bottom order
below.

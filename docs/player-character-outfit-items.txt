TAIAO — ILLUSTRATED INDEX OF OUTFIT ITEMS
======================================================
An index of every distinct item of clothing, armour, and accessory that appears
across the player-character outfits (see player-character-outfits.txt). Each
entry describes the ITEM itself in detail — its material, form, colour, cut and
trim — as one specific, concrete piece, so it can be recreated exactly. The
[bracketed] name is only the character the item is drawn from, not part of the
description. Held equipment (weapons / implements / instruments) is indexed last.


-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

-----------------------------------------------------------------------------

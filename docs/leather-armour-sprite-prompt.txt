TAIAO — LEATHER ARMOUR MATRIX PROMPTS (two sheets, 160 icons)
=========================================================================
The full tiered leather-armour set (js/skills/leathercraft.js, "TIERED LEATHER
ARMOUR"): a 5-piece set for EACH of the 32 leather tiers = 160 inventory icons.
Laid out as a MATRIX: one ROW per armour PIECE (5), COLUMNS = the LEATHER TIER,
tier 1 -> N left to right. Within a row the piece SHAPE/CUT is identical; only
the leather's colour + finish changes across the row.

Because 32 columns is too wide for one legible sheet, split at the tier midpoint
into TWO sheets, each 16 columns x 5 rows = 80 icons (matches the shield sheets):
  • SHEET 1 = leather tiers  1..16  (Rabbit … Croc)
  • SHEET 2 = leather tiers 17..32  (Ape … Leviathan)
Cells are 64x64 px (downscaled to 32x32 in game). Rows are ALWAYS in this order,
top to bottom: 1 COIF, 2 BODY, 3 CHAPS, 4 GLOVES, 5 BRACERS.

FINISH FAMILIES (applies to the tint, NOT the cut — the garment shape per row is
identical across all 32 tiers):
  • FURRED pelts (rabbit/rat/cat/fox/goat/sheep/badger/wolf/elk/hyena/ape/bear/
    lion/tiger/polar-bear/werewolf/yeti/owlbear): supple matte leather with a
    soft napped/furry trim at the edges and seams.
  • HIDE leathers (boar/deer/cow/zebra/camel/rhino/hippo): smooth worked leather,
    faint grain.
  • SCALED hides (croc/chimera/manticore/hydra/wyrm/dragon/leviathan): fine
    reptilian scale texture with a glossy sheen; the exotic top tiers may carry a
    faint magical glow but keep the same silhouette.

##############################################################################
PROMPT 1 — LEATHER ARMOUR, TIERS 1–16   (Grid: 16 cols x 5 rows = 80 icons)
##############################################################################

A transparent-background pixel-art sprite sheet of 80 leather-armour inventory
icons in 16-bit / 'HD-2D' pixel-art style — the look of roguelike NES RPGs and
classic RuneScape item sprites. Uniform grid of 16 columns x 5 rows, 64x64 px per
cell, one armour piece per cell, even margins, NO gridlines drawn. Each piece is
drawn FRONT-ON (a hair of 3/4 so the form reads), centred and filling most of its
cell, with a bold readable silhouette, a thin dark outline, a limited flat palette
with light dithered shading, soft light from the upper-left, and visible stitched
seams and buckles/laces where the piece calls for it. Fully transparent
background, no drop shadows, text, numbers, labels or frames.

Each ROW is ONE armour piece; the CUT stays IDENTICAL across that row and only the
LEATHER changes, left to right, in this exact 16-tier order — each instantly
tellable by its colour and finish (furred pelts have a soft napped trim, worked
hides are smooth, scaled hides show fine glossy scales):
  1 Rabbit — soft warm tan · 2 Rat — dull grey-brown · 3 Cat — muted slate-grey
  with faint tabby · 4 Fox — warm russet-orange · 5 Boar — mid chestnut brown ·
  6 Goat — pale sandy fawn · 7 Sheep — creamy off-white fleece · 8 Cow — reddish-
  brown cowhide · 9 Badger — dark grey-brown, silvery cast · 10 Wolf — steely
  blue-grey · 11 Elk — deep walnut brown · 12 Zebra — off-white, faint dark
  banding · 13 Hyena — dusty tan, dark mottling · 14 Camel — golden sand-tan ·
  15 Croc — olive-green glossy scales · 16 Ape — dark umber brown.

The five rows, top to bottom:
  ROW 1 — COIF: a leather head-cap / hood — a close-fitting skullcap with short
    cheek and neck flaps and a laced or buckled chin strap, front-on.
  ROW 2 — BODY: a sleeved leather cuirass / jerkin — a torso body-armour covering
    the chest, both shoulders (small shoulder guards) and both upper arms, with a
    laced or buckled front and a belt at the waist. The largest, most detailed
    piece; fills the cell.
  ROW 3 — CHAPS: leather leg armour — a matched pair of thigh-to-shin leggings
    (chaps / greaves) shown side by side, buckled straps down the sides.
  ROW 4 — GLOVES: a matched pair of leather gauntlets / gloves shown side by side,
    stitched fingers and a short flared wrist cuff.
  ROW 5 — BRACERS: a matched pair of leather wrist bracers / vambraces shown side
    by side, laced across the front, narrower and simpler than the gloves.

##############################################################################
INTEGRATION
##############################################################################
Slice each sheet row-major into 64px cells and repoint the placeholder icons the
generator makes (i_<kind>_<leatherId>) to real atlas cells. Item ids follow
<kind>_<leatherId>, where kind is one of coif/body/chaps/gloves/bracers and
leatherId is "leather" for tier 5 (Boar, i=4) or "leather_<i>" otherwise
(i = 0..31, the content.js/HIDES order = the column order above).

  ROWS  (top→bottom): 0 coif, 1 body, 2 chaps, 3 gloves, 4 bracers
  SHEET 1 columns c=0..15  -> tier i = c        (leatherId: i===4?"leather":"leather_"+i)
  SHEET 2 columns c=0..15  -> tier i = c + 16    (leatherId: "leather_"+i)

So cell (row r, col c) on sheet 1 = SPR key for ITEMS["<ROWS[r]>_<leatherId(c)>"].icon,
and sheet 2 the same with i=c+16. Register both sheets in js/sprites (a new
leather-armour-icons-data.js, mirroring shield-icons-data.js) and set each item's
.icon after leathercraft.js/geartiers.js have defined the items (load order per
index.html). The two mi-icons hooks that currently point body_leather and
body_leather_31 at mx_leather_body / mx_master_cuirass can be dropped once these
dedicated icons exist.

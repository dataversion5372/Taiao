TAIAO — PAULDRON ICON PROMPT
========================================
AUDIT: Smithing's tiered armour (geartiers.js ARMOR_KINDS) forges a "Pauldrons"
piece for each of the 16 ARMOUR METALS (ids pauldrons_<metal>, offhand shoulder
slots), but they all fall back to a single hue-tinted i_shield sprite — so all
16 pauldrons are the same silhouette in a different colour. The chestplate, legs,
helm and boots already have real per-metal art (assets/assorted sprites/
armour.png); this sheet gives Pauldrons the matching per-metal line: 16 shoulder
pieces, one per armour metal, to pair with the existing chest/legs art.

==============================================================================
GLOBAL STYLE
==============================================================================
Art style: 16-bit / 'HD-2D' pixel-art inventory icons in the look of the
roguelike NES RPGs and classic RuneScape armour sprites (match the game's
existing helm/chestplate/legs/boots armour icons). One item per cell: a matched
PAIR of pauldrons — two articulated shoulder guards of overlapping lames with a
domed shoulder cap, shown 3/4 and slightly angled so both read, sitting side by
side (or one overlapping the other). Bold readable silhouette, thin dark
outline, a limited flat palette with light dithered shading, and soft light from
the upper-left giving the metal a domed sheen. Riveted lame edges where the metal
calls for it; leave any heraldic field blank. Fully TRANSPARENT background
(alpha), no drop shadows, no text, numbers, labels, borders or frames. Consistent
scale, framing and lighting across the whole sheet so the 16 icons read as one
matched set — every cell the SAME pauldron shape, only the METAL changing.

Each cell is ONE armour metal; the pauldron shape stays IDENTICAL and only the
metal changes, left-to-right then top-to-bottom, in this exact 16-metal order —
each instantly tellable by its colour and finish:
  1 Bronze — warm reddish-brown · 2 Zinc — cool blue-white · 3 Lead — dull dark
  blue-grey · 4 Nickel — pale greenish-silver · 5 Titanium — grey with a faint
  teal sheen · 6 Mithril — pale glowing blue · 7 Orichalcum — rose-gold ·
  8 Starmetal — deep navy flecked with silver stars · 9 Frostiron — icy pale
  blue-white rime · 10 Duskmetal — twilight violet-grey · 11 Dawnmetal — warm
  gold-pink · 12 Chronite — brassy clockwork-gold · 13 Voidforged steel —
  purple-black with a dark sheen · 14 Twilight alloy — dusky violet-grey with a
  faint glow · 15 Nickel silver — bright white-silver · 16 Cupronickel — warm
  pale gold-silver.

Output: one PNG sprite sheet, a uniform grid of 8 columns x 2 rows, 64x64 px per
cell (draw at 64x64; the game downscales to 32x32), one icon per cell, even
margins, no gridlines drawn, in the EXACT order above (cells 1-8 top row,
9-16 bottom row).

INTEGRATION (after the PNG is generated):
  - Wire the art like shield-icons-data.js: a self-registering
    js/sprites/pauldron-icons-data.js with a FREE 2-letter key (existing: aa ab
    ag ah al aw bi cb cf cg ch ci co cp fg fi fs ft fu hb hi jw mb mi ml ms nl
    sh tg wg wt — e.g. "pd" or "pl"), SHEET_TILE[key]=64, slice row-major into
    64px cells, register SPR and repoint ITEMS[pauldrons_<metal>].icon.
  - Column/cell order = the ARMOUR_BARS roster (geartiers.js):
    cell 0 bronze, 1 m3, 2 m4, 3 m6, 4 m11, 5 m12, 6 m13, 7 m20, 8 m22, 9 m25,
    10 m26, 11 m30, 12 voidforged, 13 twilight, 14 nickel_silver, 15 cupronickel
    → item id "pauldrons_" + that shortBar.
  - Include in index.html AFTER js/skills/geartiers.js so the pauldrons_* ITEMS
    exist; the repoint then overrides the i_shield fallback.

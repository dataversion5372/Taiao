TAIAO — SHIELD ICON PROMPTS
=======================================
AUDIT: the tiered gear system (geartiers.js) forges eight armour pieces for
each of the 16 ARMOUR METALS — helm, boots, chestplate, legs, chainbody,
pauldrons, plate arms, gauntlets — but NO shield. The only shields in the
game are three one-offs (wooden_shield, iron_shield, mithril_shield). These
sheets add a real per-metal shield line: one sheet per shield KIND, 16 cells
(one metal each, in ARMOUR METALS roster order), same as every other armour
sheet. Integration follows the "gi" pattern (tools/build_item_icons.py ->
item-icons-data.js): slice each sheet into 64px cells left-to-right / top-to-
bottom, pack, and repoint each shield family's per-metal ITEMS[id].icon. In
the game each sheet becomes an equip:"shield" ARMOR_KIND in geartiers.js
(ids like kiteshield_<shortBar>, block scaling with tier), forged at the anvil.

==============================================================================
GLOBAL STYLE (paste above every sheet prompt)
==============================================================================
Art style: 16-bit / 'HD-2D' pixel-art inventory icons in the look of the
roguelike NES RPGs and classic RuneScape item sprites. One shield per cell,
centred and filling most of the cell, drawn FRONT-ON (face toward the viewer,
maybe a hair of 3/4 so the boss/curve reads), with a bold readable silhouette,
a thin dark outline, a limited flat palette with a little dithered shading,
and a soft light from the upper-left giving the metal a domed sheen. Riveted
rims, a raised central boss and faint hammer-planished texture where the kind
calls for it; leave the heraldic field blank (no painted crests or emblems
beyond the shape's own studwork/banding). Fully TRANSPARENT background (alpha),
no drop shadows, no text, numbers, labels, borders or frames. Output: one PNG
per sheet, a uniform grid of 64x64 px cells (drawn at 64x64, downscaled to
32x32 in game), one icon per cell, even margins, no gridlines drawn.

Within a sheet the SHIELD SHAPE stays IDENTICAL between cells — only the METAL
changes, and each metal must be tellable at a glance by colour and finish.

==============================================================================
SHEETS — one per shield kind, ARMOUR METALS roster, Grid: 4 cols x 4 rows (16)
==============================================================================
MAJOR / MARTIAL SHIELDS
  SHEET Heater shields   — the classic knight's shield ("square" shield):
     flat straight top edge, straight sides tapering to a rounded point at the
     bottom, a subtle raised vertical spine down the centre, riveted rim.

  SHEET Kite shields     — tall Norman kite/almond shield: broad rounded top
     narrowing to a long point at the bottom (teardrop), a central vertical
     ridge, riveted edging, a small round rivet-boss near the top.

  SHEET Round shields    — Viking-style circular shield: radial plank/plate
     banding, a domed central iron boss, a studded reinforced rim.

  SHEET Bucklers         — small circular fist-shield: dominated by a strong
     domed central boss, a narrow reinforced rim, gripped from behind (no
     straps shown); reads noticeably smaller/lighter than the round shield.

  SHEET Tower shields    — tall rectangular shield: full-height rounded-corner
     rectangle with a gentle vertical convex curve, two or three horizontal
     reinforcing bands and corner rivets; heavy and imposing.

CULTURAL SHIELDS
  SHEET Aspis (hoplon)   — large deep-dished ancient-Greek round shield: a
     broad flat offset rim ring around a domed bowl, a single small central
     boss, clean classical lines.

  SHEET Scutum (Roman)   — large convex rectangular legionary shield: curved
     to wrap the body, a horizontal spindle-shaped central iron boss (umbo),
     vertical reinforcing spine top-to-bottom, rounded top and bottom edges.

  SHEET Targe (Scottish) — round highland shield: concentric rings of tooled
     brass studwork and rosettes radiating from a small central domed boss
     (with an optional short central spike), a studded rim.

  SHEET Chimalli (Aztec) — round Mesoamerican war shield: concentric radial
     colour bands and a stepped-fret (greca) motif around a central boss, a
     suggested fringe of feathers along the lower rim; the METAL is the plated
     face and boss, the banding reads as inlay.

  SHEET Dhal (Indian)    — small convex round shield: four evenly-spaced domed
     bosses set in a square around the centre, a decorated raised rim, a
     shallow overall dome.

  SHEET Pavise           — tall crossbowman's standing shield: a full-height
     rounded-rectangle with a pronounced vertical central gutter/ridge running
     its whole length (a shallow "roof" fold), flat riveted face, broad and
     flat-bottomed so it could stand on the ground.

INTEGRATION (after each PNG is generated)
  - Add each sheet to tools/build_item_icons.py in the shields group, keeping
    the 16-cell ARMOUR METALS order; it packs 64px cells into item-icons.
  - In geartiers.js, add one equip:"shield" entry per kind to ARMOR_KINDS
    (e.g. ["kiteshield","Kite shield", share, barQty, "i_shield", greater,
    ["shield"]]); the loop auto-forges <kind>_<metal> for all 16 metals with
    block scaling by tier. Repoint each to its sheet cell; retire the two
    stray one-offs (iron_shield/mithril_shield) or fold them into the round/
    heater lines.

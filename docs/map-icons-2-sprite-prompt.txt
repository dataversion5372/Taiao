MAP ICON SPRITESHEET #2 — image generation prompt
==================================================
Purpose: NEW dedicated world-map marker icons for the shops and specialised
crafting stations that currently have no icon of their own. Today they fall
back to a generic glyph — e.g. every fletcher's bench, sawmill, cooperage,
tailor's bench and bindery shows the plain "workbench" icon; brewery, dyeworks,
creamery and soap works all show the "cauldron"; malthouse, pottery kiln, glass
furnace, lime kiln, charcoal clamp and assay furnace all show the "furnace";
masons' yard, toolsmith, locksmith, drawbench all show the "anvil"; leather /
cobbler / saddler benches all show the "tanning rack"; and every named shop on
the map shows the same "store" pouch. This sheet gives each its own silhouette.

Same pipeline and art style as docs/map-icons-sprite-prompt.txt (the "mi" sheet):
one combined spritesheet, extracted by fixed grid position afterward — do NOT
rely on labels for identification. Rendered at 18x18px on the map and 10x10px on
the minimap, so every icon must read as a bold chunky silhouette when tiny.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------
PROMPT (send as one single image-generation request)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------

Create ONE flat 2D game-icon spritesheet: a precise 6-column by 7-row grid of
42 equal square cells, with generous even padding between cells so nothing
touches or overlaps its neighbors. Every cell has the exact same flat, solid,
uniform magenta background color (#FF00FF) with zero gradient, texture, or
shadow in the background — this magenta is a chroma-key color to be removed
later, so it must NOT appear anywhere in the icon artwork itself. Do not draw
grid lines, cell borders, labels, numbers, or captions anywhere in the image.

Art style for every icon: simple, bold, flat-shaded fantasy RPG minimap/
world-map marker icon, similar to OSRS-style map icons — chunky readable
silhouette, thick clean black outline (this outline is important, keep it
consistent on every icon), 3-6 flat solid colors per icon (no gradients, no
soft shading, no photorealism), designed to stay legible when scaled down to
18x18 pixels. Every icon centered in its cell and sized consistently relative
to the others (similar visual weight/scale, none tiny and none overflowing).

Fill the 42 cells, reading left-to-right then top-to-bottom, with exactly these
icons in exactly this order:

Row 1 — woodworking stations: (1) FLETCHER'S BENCH: a half-carved wooden bow
with a white feathered arrow and a small knife/shaving curl. (2) SAWMILL: a
circular-ish crosscut hand saw with a grey blade and brown handle biting into a
brown log. (3) COOPERAGE: a brown wooden barrel with two grey metal hoop bands
and a couple of curved staves leaning beside it. (4) ROPEWALK: a neat coil of
tan/brown twisted rope. (5) SAIL LOFT: a triangular cream ship's sail on a
short brown spar/boom. (6) SHIPYARD: the curved wooden ribbed hull of a boat
under construction on a slipway.

Row 2 — textile & leather stations: (7) SPINNING WHEEL: a brown spinning wheel
with visible spokes and a small spindle, a wisp of cream thread. (8) DYEWORKS: a
squat open vat with brightly colored dye (split so it reads as two colors, e.g.
blue and red) and a couple of drips down the side. (9) FULLING MILL: a wooden
trough/tub with folded pale cloth being pressed, a trickle of water. (10)
TAILOR'S BENCH: a spool of thread with a large silver sewing needle stuck
through it and a short trailing thread. (11) LEATHER BENCH: a folded tan hide/
piece of leather with a curved awl or half-moon leather knife on top. (12)
COBBLER'S BENCH: a single brown leather boot/shoe on a small last, with a tiny
hammer.

Row 3 — leather & husbandry: (13) SADDLER'S BENCH: a brown horse saddle in
profile with a visible stirrup. (14) BARN (husbandry): a red barn building with
a peaked roof and a pale X-braced double door. (15) CREAMERY: a wooden butter
churn (tall tapering tub) with a plunger handle sticking out the top, a small
white milk splash. (16) CHANDLERY (candle maker): two upright cream candles,
one with a small orange flame, dipped from a rack. (17) SOAP WORKS: a stack of
two or three cream/pale soap bars with a few round white bubbles. (18) PAPER
MILL: a stacked sheaf of pale cream paper sheets, top sheet lifting/curling.

Row 4 — heat & fire stations (make each clearly distinct from the plain grey
furnace on the first sheet): (19) MALTHOUSE: a low kiln floor with pale golden
grain/barley heaped on it and a soft warm glow beneath. (20) BREWERY: a wooden
beer barrel with a tap/spigot and a foaming tankard beside it. (21) CHARCOAL
CLAMP: a rounded black earthen charcoal mound with thin grey smoke wisps and a
small dull-orange glow at its base. (22) LIME KILN: a stout stone bottle-shaped
kiln with an arched mouth and a pale-orange glow inside, a puff of white smoke.
(23) POTTERY KILN: a rounded brick kiln with a terracotta clay pot visible in
its glowing mouth. (24) GLASS FURNACE: a furnace mouth with a glowing molten
orange glass blob on the end of a long grey blowpipe.

Row 5 — metal & stone stations (make each clearly distinct from the plain grey
anvil on the first sheet): (25) ASSAY FURNACE: a small crucible tipping a stream
of bright molten gold, with one gold bar/ingot. (26) DRAWBENCH: a long bench
drawing a thin silver metal wire through a die-plate, the wire spooling. (27)
JEWELLER'S BENCH: a gold ring set with a bright blue faceted gem, plus tiny
sparkle. (28) TOOLSMITH: a crossed grey hammer and a pair of pincers/tongs. (29)
LOCKSMITH BENCH: a brass padlock with a gold key crossing over it. (30) MASONS'
YARD: a squared grey stone block with a chisel and mallet resting on it.

Row 6 — named SHOP markers (each a small shop stall/awning silhouette themed by
trade, so they read as shops rather than stations): (31) WOODCUTTER'S SHOP: a
market stall with a brown-striped awning displaying an axe. (32) MINING
SUPPLIES: a market stall awning displaying a pickaxe. (33) FISHMONGER: a market
stall awning displaying a blue fish (fish on a slab). (34) ARMOURY: a market
stall awning displaying a grey chestplate/breastplate of armour. (35) SEED
MERCHANT: a market stall awning displaying a small brown sack spilling pale
seeds. (36) TIMBER YARD: a market stall awning displaying a stack of cut brown
planks/boards.

Row 7 — spares: (37) leave completely empty, (38) leave completely empty, (39)
leave completely empty, (40) leave completely empty, (41) leave completely
empty, (42) leave completely empty (each nothing but the plain magenta
background).

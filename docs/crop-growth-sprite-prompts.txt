ISLE OF EMBERFALL — CROP GROWTH-STAGE REFERENCE SHEETS
One sheet per agriculture skill (Cerealiculture/Olericulture/Pomiculture/
Herbiculture/Fibriculture), each covering all 32 of that skill's crops
across 4 growth stages. These are the IN-FIELD PLANT sprites (what's
actually planted in a farm plot), NOT inventory icons — the seed and
harvested-good icons for these same 32-crop lists already exist as flat
item icons (see docs/sprite-generation-prompts.txt); this is the missing
piece, since farm plots currently render every crop with one of only two
generic placeholder plant sprites regardless of what's actually planted.
Same labelled-reference-chart convention as assets/assorted sprites/
Tools.png / weapons.png / armour.png / ingots.png / arrows.png, so it can
go through the same extraction pipeline once generated.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GLOBAL STYLE (applies to all 5 sheets below)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pixel-art front-on farm-plot plant sprites in an HD-2D RPG farming-sim style
(Stardew-Valley-like growth staging, rendered in the same crisp pixel-art
look as the rest of this game's world sprites). Each plant is shown growing
directly out of tilled brown soil, viewed from a 3/4 top-down angle matching
an overhead farm plot. Fully transparent background around each sprite
(alpha, no soil/background baked in beyond a small mound of dirt right at
the base of the stem/roots where it meets the "ground" — trim the dirt
close, don't draw a full soil tile square). No drop shadows beyond a soft
contact shadow at the base. No text, numbers, gridlines or borders baked
into the individual sprites themselves — only the sheet-level labels below.
Consistent scale and light source (soft light from upper-left) across every
sprite on every sheet, so they read as one coherent set once cropped out.

Every crop is shown across exactly 4 growth stages, always in this order:
  Stage 1 — Seedling: a tiny fresh sprout just breaking the soil, one or two
    small pale-green leaves, mostly bare dirt still visible.
  Stage 2 — Growing: a young leafy plant, noticeably bigger, healthy green,
    no fruit/grain/flower yet, still clearly immature.
  Stage 3 — Mature: full-sized foliage, budding or flowering, the first hint
    of colour from whatever the plant will produce, but not yet ripe.
  Stage 4 — Ready to Harvest: fully grown and ripe/laden — the plant at its
    most visually distinct and colourful, obviously ready to pick (fruit,
    grain heads, bulbs, or fibre stalks clearly visible and mature-coloured).

Layout: a 4-column x 8-row grid of 32 crop GROUPS (one group per crop, in
the exact order listed per sheet below). Each group is its own labelled
block: the crop's name centred above in small bold white pixel-font text on
the dark background, and below the label a horizontal strip of its 4 growth
stages left-to-right in the order above, each stage a small distinct sprite
with a little even spacing between stages and between groups (no gridlines
needed — spacing alone separates blocks, matching the Tools.png reference
sheet's layout). Background of the whole sheet: solid black (or very dark
neutral), so the transparent per-sprite regions crop out cleanly. At the
very bottom of the sheet, add a small labelled legend row showing the 4
stage names in order ("Seedling", "Growing", "Mature", "Ready to Harvest")
under one example plant, confirming the stage order used in every group
above (same legend-row convention as the Tools.png / arrows.png sheets).

ISLE OF EMBERFALL — CULTURAL GARMENT ICONS (one sheet, 48 icons)
===============================================================
Inventory icons for the 48 cultural garments of the Tailoring wardrobe
(js/skills/garments.js) — the soft-cloth clothing worn across the 223-character
roster (docs/player-character-outfits.txt). ONE garment per cell, laid out as a
uniform 8-COLUMN x 6-ROW grid of 64x64 px cells (downscaled to 32x32 in game),
even margins, NO gridlines. Row-major reading order EXACTLY matches the item
order below so the sheet can be sliced and repointed onto i_<id> mechanically.

STYLE: 16-bit / 'HD-2D' pixel-art, the look of roguelike NES RPGs and classic
RuneScape item sprites. Each garment is drawn as a neat FRONT-ON "flat-lay"
inventory icon (a hair of 3/4 where it helps read), centred and filling most of
its cell, with a bold readable silhouette, a thin dark outline, a limited flat
palette with light dithered shading and soft light from the upper-left. Show
stitched seams, hems, trim, buttons, laces and folds where the piece calls for
it. Fully transparent background — no drop shadows, mannequins, bodies, text,
numbers, labels or frames (garments hang/lay empty, no wearer).

##############################################################################
INTEGRATION
##############################################################################
Single sheet, 8 cols x 6 rows, 64px cells, row-major. Slice into 64px cells and
repoint each item's placeholder icon (i_<id>, made by garments.js/defineIcon) to
its cell. The item ids in row-major order are exactly:

  wizard_hat, flat_cap, hood, cowl, cloth_coif, turban, desert_headwrap, feather_headdress,
  mage_robe, sorcerer_robe, cleric_vestment, scholar_robe, travelers_robe, ceremonial_robe, djinn_robe, monk_robe,
  tunic, cloth_jerkin, quilted_vest, frock_coat, greatcoat, linen_dress, peasant_dress, tabard,
  kimono, hanbok, hanfu, poncho, boubou, parka, buckskin_dress, sari,
  cloak, mantle, stole, feathered_cloak, fur_mantle, moss_cloak, hooded_cloak, half_cape,
  kilt, skirt, leggings, loincloth, arm_wraps, sash, cloth_belt, cloth_apron

Register the sheet in a new js/sprites/garment-icons-data.js that self-registers
its key in SHEET_KEYS/SHEET_TILE (tile 64) and ASSET_DATA, then sets
SPR["i_"+id] = [sheet,0,0,{sx:(k%8)*64, sy:Math.floor(k/8)*64, sw:64, sh:64}] for
each id at row-major index k — exactly the flow used by
js/sprites/leather-armour-icons-data.js. Source art on a black background should
be border-flood-filled to transparent, gridded and resampled to 64px cells first.
